clips

A personal desktop tool for short-form video.

clips is a self-hosted desktop application used by its owner to select short moments from video files, render them in vertical format with captions, and upload them to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

It has a single user — its owner — and is not offered to the public. Every upload is triggered manually. Account tokens are stored only on the owner's computer; no data is collected from anyone else and nothing is shared with third parties.

How it works

  • The owner signs in once with their own TikTok account (Login Kit).
  • The tool renders a clip and uploads it as a draft to the owner's TikTok inbox (Content Posting API), or posts it directly with a chosen privacy level.
  • The owner reviews and publishes from the TikTok app.
