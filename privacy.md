# Privacy Policy — clips

_Last updated: 20 September 2026_

**clips** is a personal, self-hosted desktop tool used by its owner to prepare
short video clips and publish them to the owner's own social media accounts.
It is not offered to the public and has a single user: its owner.

## What data the tool handles

- **Account authorization.** When the owner connects a TikTok or YouTube
  account, the platform issues OAuth access and refresh tokens. These tokens
  are stored **only on the owner's own computer** in a local file and are used
  solely to upload videos to that account and to read the status and view
  counts of those uploads.
- **Basic profile information** (`user.info.basic`): display name and avatar,
  shown in the tool's interface to confirm which account is connected.
- **Video files** created by the owner, which are uploaded to the connected
  account at the owner's explicit request.

## What the tool does not do

- It does not collect, store or process data of any person other than its owner.
- It does not send any data to third parties. There are no analytics,
  advertising or tracking components.
- It does not run on a server; nothing is stored outside the owner's machine.
- It does not post, like, comment or follow on the owner's behalf except for
  the video uploads the owner explicitly triggers.

## Data retention and deletion

All tokens and cached data live in the tool's local `data` folder. Deleting
that folder — or revoking the app's access in the TikTok or YouTube account
settings — removes the tool's access completely and immediately.

## Third-party platforms

Use of TikTok and YouTube through this tool is subject to their own terms and
privacy policies:

- TikTok: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy
- YouTube / Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
